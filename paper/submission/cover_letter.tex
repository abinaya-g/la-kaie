\opening{Dear Editors,}

I submit the manuscript \emph{``Does the learning matter? A pre-registered deconstruction of SARSA-guided information
exchange in the MPHBS hybrid metaheuristic''} for consideration as a research article in the \emph{Journal of
Heuristics}.

Learned controllers are increasingly built into metaheuristics, and their benefit is usually demonstrated by an
ablation against a random control. The manuscript examines when such an ablation measures the effect of learning, and
applies the answer to a recent, fully documented case: MPHBS (Flores and Olivares, \emph{Knowledge-Based Systems} 352,
2026), a Honey Badger--Marine Predators hybrid whose information exchange is guided by a SARSA value table. It is, in
the tradition of this journal, a study of which components of a heuristic actually carry its performance.

The main contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item a decomposition of any learned-versus-control contrast into a learning, a budget and a state term, with the
  conditions under which a control measures learning alone;
\item a proof, checked against the authors' code and per-run results, that the published random control runs 21\%
  (CEC2022) and 16\% (FIR) fewer iterations at the same budget and, in one configuration, changes a population memory;
\item a pre-registered study with matched controls (14\,300 seed-paired runs on CEC2022 and eight FIR filter-design
  problems), analysed with a script frozen before the campaign: a random mediator performs at least as well as SARSA,
  while the exchange step and the memory handling carry the performance;
\item an explanation from the update rule itself, with two propositions on credit assignment and on the noise floor of
  the value table, confirmed by logged runs: on one benchmark there is no stable signal to learn, and on the other the
  table ranks a stable signal in reverse;
\item an extension to the 24 BBOB functions and a protocol for constructing and verifying matched controls.
\end{itemize}

The findings are negative for the learned component but practical for the field: they identify what drives the hybrid
and give a checkable standard for ablating learned components. All code, the frozen pre-registration, the raw runs and
the analysis scripts are publicly available.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication
elsewhere. The author declares no competing interests, and the research received no specific funding.

Thank you for considering this submission.

\closing{Yours sincerely,}
